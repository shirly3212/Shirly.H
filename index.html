About

Curious by nature. Precise by craft.

I'm a curious Creative Designer who loves exploring the intersection between design, motion, and technology.

With years of experience in fast-paced media environments, I create visual experiences that combine storytelling, precision, and innovation.

I use AI as a creative partner to explore ideas, improve workflows, and push visual possibilities.

I'm constantly learning new technologies, including coding and creative automation, to solve design problems in smarter ways.
