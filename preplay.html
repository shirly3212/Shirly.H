02 · Motion Design Experience

PrePlay

A motion identity built to carry rhythm and energy across broadcast, social, and on-platform formats — where the story has to land in seconds.

RoleMotion Designer
FocusMotion Graphics
FormatBroadcast + Social

Project Overview

Motion as a brand signature.

PrePlay needed a movement language distinct enough to be recognized muted, mid-scroll, in a fraction of a second. The work centers on a small set of motion principles applied consistently rather than a library of one-off effects.

My Role

Motion Design & Animation

  • Motion system design
  • Frame-by-frame animation in After Effects
  • Edit and pacing in Premiere
  • Sound-to-motion synchronization

Creative Process

Building a repeatable rhythm.

Reference→ Rhythm Study→ Storyboard→ Animation→ Sound Pass→ Final Cut

Every sequence was tested muted first — if the pacing didn't read without sound, it wasn't ready.

Reflection

What travels, and what doesn't.

PrePlay reinforced that a motion identity only works if it survives being cut down — the same principles had to hold at fifteen seconds and at ninety.
